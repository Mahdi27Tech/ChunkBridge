Send files that are too big to send.

ChunkBridge cuts a big file into parts, you send the parts on WhatsApp or Telegram, and the other phone puts them back together exactly. No server, no data bundle for the app itself, and nothing is uploaded.

Download the app (APK) Read the user manual

Free · Android 8.0 or newer · works offline

Every part and the finished file are checked with SHA-256, so a damaged copy is caught instead of saved.

How it works

Split

Pick any file and a part size. ChunkBridge creates numbered parts plus a small manifest that describes them.

Send

Share the manifest first, then the parts, through any app. You can pause and continue later.

Rebuild

The other phone adds the manifest and parts, sees what is missing, and rebuilds the original file.

Install the app

ChunkBridge is installed from an APK file, so both phones need it.

  1. Tap Download the app (APK) above and download the latest file.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps.
  3. Tap Install. Because the app is not on Google Play, Android may show a warning. Continue only for a file you downloaded from this page.
  4. Open ChunkBridge. It needs no accounts and no special permissions.

User manual

Sending a file

  1. Open Split a file and tap Choose file.
  2. Pick a part size. Use a preset or type your own in MB. Keep it below what your messaging app allows.
  3. Optional: switch on Protect with a password. Tell the receiver the password separately, never in the same chat as the parts.
  4. Tap Start splitting and keep the app open until it says Split complete.
  5. Tap Share chunks. Send the manifest first, then use Share next chunk until every part is sent.

Receiving a file

  1. Save the manifest and the parts from the chat to your phone.
  2. Open Receive a file and tap Choose manifest.
  3. Tap Add chunks and select the parts. Any order, any number at a time.
  4. Check the map. Missing parts are listed, so you know exactly what to ask for again.
  5. Enter the password if the transfer is encrypted, then tap Rebuild the file.
  6. Use Share / save the file to move the result to your gallery, Files or another app.

Good to know

  • Splitting and rebuilding need free space of roughly twice the file size while they work.
  • For very large files keep the app open and the phone charged until the job finishes.
  • History lists past transfers. Open one to keep sharing the parts you have not sent yet.
  • Settings lets you change the size presets and switch between light and dark themes.

Questions

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. ChunkBridge has no server and no accounts. Files stay on your phone until you share them yourself.

A part arrived damaged or is missing. What now?

The Receive screen names the corrupted or missing parts. Ask the sender to send only those parts again, add them, and rebuild.

The parts got renamed by WhatsApp or Telegram. Is that a problem?

No. Parts are recognised by their content, not their names, so renamed copies still work.

I forgot the password.

The file cannot be recovered. Encryption is real: without the password nobody, including the developer, can open it.

Does the receiver need the app?

Yes. The receiver installs ChunkBridge to rebuild the file. The sender only shares ordinary files.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. ChunkBridge is for Android.

The rebuild says it is out of space.

Free up space and try again. Your imported parts are kept, and nothing half-finished is left behind.
